📖 How to Play

  • The game has 5 levels about DNA biology.
  • In each level, drag and drop items to the correct spots.
  • You have a timer per level — complete before time runs out!
  • Use 💡 hints (3 available) if you're stuck.
  • Click ✔ Check Answer to submit your answer.
  • Earn bonus points for speed and accuracy!
  • Complete all 5 levels to earn your certificate. 🎓
Touch devices: Tap items and then tap a slot to place them.
